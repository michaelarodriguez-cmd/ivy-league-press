ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

Independent by design.

Ivy League Press is an independent digital news project in development. Its goal is to produce accurate, accessible reporting on government, politics and the exercise of power. This website is a launch prototype; it does not yet represent an active newsroom or a portfolio of published investigations.

Ivy League Press is not affiliated with the Ivy League athletic conference or any of its member universities.

Editorial Commitments

Verify before publishing

Distinguish fact and opinion

Clearly label analysis, commentary and reporting.
