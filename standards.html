Editorial Standards

Our proposed commitments for future reporting.

Accuracy and verification

Verify material claims through reliable sources, favor primary documents, and distinguish verified facts from allegations or opinion.

Fairness

Seek a meaningful response from subjects of serious criticism before publication.

Corrections

Correct substantive errors promptly and transparently with dated notices.

Independence

Disclose relevant conflicts and clearly label advertisements, sponsored content and opinion.

Source protection

Do not promise confidentiality through ordinary email. A secure tip system must be established before sensitive submissions are invited.
